About me

I'm a BS Data Science & Applications student at IIT Madras, with a completed BSc in Chemistry behind me. I like problems where the model is only half the job — getting the data right, measuring honestly, and putting the result in front of real users.

My recent work: fine-tuning an AudioSet-pretrained Audio Spectrogram Transformer to a 0.825 macro F1 on a noisy, stem-mixed genre dataset, building a hybrid-retrieval RAG chatbot for PDFs with its own eval harness,, and shipping full-stack apps with Vue, Flask, Celery and Docker.

Outside ML, I do cybersecurity research and bug bounty hunting, which taught me to think about how systems break, not just how they work.
